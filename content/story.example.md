# Why this field

What drew you to this kind of work, and what keeps you in it. Specific beats grand:
the project, the moment, the problem you could not stop thinking about. Searching in
more than one field? Write a paragraph for each, so a letter can use the one that fits.

# What I'm looking for next

What you want to own, learn, or build in your next role, and why now.

# Career moves, in my words

- From your first role to your second: what you went looking for, and what you found.
- Each move after that, the same way.

# Stories worth telling

One paragraph per story that expands a resume bullet: the situation, what you did,
and what changed because of it. These are what an evidence paragraph is built from.

# How I write

Optional. A short paragraph you wrote yourself -- an email, a post, a note to a
colleague -- so letters can sound like you.

# Keep out of letters

- Anything a letter should never mention, named by topic, not the details: "Why I
  left Acme."
