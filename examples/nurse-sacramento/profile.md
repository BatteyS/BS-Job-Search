# Career Profile — Jordan Rivera (fictional example)

This file is what /job-scan scores against. It is written for the scorer, not for an
employer, so it can be candid: what Jordan wants, what they will not take, and how to
tell a good role from a merely matching one. /setup writes a first version from an
interview; after that it is the user's, edited by hand.

Nothing here goes into a resume or a letter. Resume facts live in `content/master.md`.

## 1. Identity and hard constraints

- **Registered nurse, BSN, CCRN.** Six years of cardiac nursing: telemetry and step-down
  2019–2021, cardiovascular ICU since 2022. Relief charge nurse. Epic super-user.
- **Location:** Sacramento metro only, at most about a 45-minute commute. Not relocating —
  family is here. Remote or hybrid is welcome for informatics and education roles.
- **Licensure:** California RN license only. Roles requiring another state's license or a
  multistate compact license are out.
- **Pay:** at least $110,000 a year, base. Hourly postings count at 2,080 hours a year.
- **Employment:** full-time or 0.9 FTE staff roles. No travel contracts, no per diem, no
  agency placements.
- **Schedule:** days preferred. Nights are acceptable for a clear step up, never permanent
  rotating shifts.

## 2. Evidence bank

The strongest evidence, for the scorer's reference. `content/master.md` has all of it.

- **Monitoring and charting projects:** led a capnography pilot across 20 beds; charting
  compliance 71% → 98% in four months; trained 45 nurses.
- **Systems work:** Epic super-user for a 2023 upgrade — tip sheets, go-live support on
  three units, 140 issues logged to the build team. Built a staffing grid two other units
  adopted.
- **Teaching:** precepted eight nurses through ICU orientation; all finished on schedule.
- **Critical care:** post-CABG, valve, LVAD, IABP, CRRT, vasoactive titration.
- **In progress:** graduate certificate in nursing informatics, finishing 2027.

## 4. Scoring rubric

### 4.1 What makes a role worth applying to

Jordan's own test: *"Does this get me closer to informatics or teaching, or is it just
another ICU job with a different badge?"*

So **direction matters more than match.** An informatics analyst role at a mid-size
hospital that wants a nurse who knows Epic beats a perfect-match CVICU posting at a
hospital Jordan would only be moving sideways to.

### 4.2 Score up

- Informatics or education work that uses Epic, workflow design, or staff training.
- Roles that state a nursing background is required or preferred for an analyst title —
  they value exactly the bridge Jordan has.
- Hospitals with a clinical ladder, tuition support (the certificate), or Magnet status.
- Hybrid or remote informatics roles inside California.
- Bedside roles only when they add charge, education, or project time.

### 4.3 Score down

- Analyst roles with no clinical component (billing, revenue cycle, lab, pharmacy builds).
- Informatics roles requiring an Epic certification *and* three or more years as an
  analyst — a reach, though still worth a look when the posting says it trains.
- Bedside roles that are lateral: same unit type, same responsibilities, no ladder.
- Night-only positions, unless the role is a clear step up.

### 4.4 Location

Sacramento metro carries no penalty. Remote roles take a small one (−3): welcome, but a
hospital Jordan can walk into is worth something for a first informatics job.

### 4.5 Hard passes

- Travel, per diem, agency, and contract roles.
- Pediatric, neonatal, and labor-and-delivery units — no experience, no interest.
- Roles needing an advanced-practice license (NP, CNS) or a master's degree Jordan does
  not have.
- Anything outside the Sacramento metro that is not remote.

## 5. Resume

One page. Licenses and certifications sit right under experience, because a hiring
manager's first question is the license. Lead with the capnography pilot for informatics
and education roles, and with critical-care depth for bedside roles.
