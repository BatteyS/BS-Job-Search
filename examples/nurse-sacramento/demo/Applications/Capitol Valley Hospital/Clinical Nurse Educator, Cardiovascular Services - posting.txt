Clinical Nurse Educator, Cardiovascular Services
Capitol Valley Hospital · Roseville, CA · Days, Monday to Friday
$59.40 – $71.25 an hour

About the role
You will be the educator for our cardiovascular ICU and cardiac step-down units: about 90
nurses across both. You will own their orientation, their annual competencies, and the
training that goes with every new practice or piece of equipment.

What you will do
- Run orientation for new graduates and transfers, and support their preceptors.
- Plan and teach annual competencies and skills days.
- Lead the education for practice changes, from the first draft of a checklist to the
  audit afterwards.
- Work with the unit's practice council on policies and workflows.

What you bring
- A current California RN license and a BSN; an MSN is preferred.
- At least three years in cardiovascular or critical care.
- CCRN, or certification within a year of hire.
- Experience precepting or teaching nurses.
