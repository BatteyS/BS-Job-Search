Clinical Informatics Nurse, Inpatient Documentation
Sierra Crest Health · Sacramento, CA · Hybrid, three days on site
$124,000 – $148,000 a year

About the role
Our four hospitals share one Epic build. The clinical informatics team turns what nurses
need at the bedside into documentation that is quick to chart and easy to find. You will
join six informatics nurses who own inpatient nursing documentation in Epic ClinDoc:
flowsheets, care plans, and order sets.

What you will do
- Gather requirements from bedside nurses and nurse managers, and translate inpatient
  nursing workflows into Epic build requests.
- Test new and changed flowsheets before each release, and write the tip sheets that go
  with them.
- Support go-lives at the elbow on every unit that receives a change.
- Train nurses and super-users on new documentation, in classes and on the unit.
- Track adoption after each change, and bring what nurses report back to the build team.

What you bring
- A current California RN license and a BSN.
- At least three years of acute-care nursing; critical care preferred.
- Experience as an Epic super-user, or supporting an Epic go-live.
- The habit of asking nurses how a workflow really works before changing it.

Nice to have
- Progress toward a nursing informatics certificate or degree.
- Charge nurse or precepting experience.

We sponsor Epic ClinDoc certification within six months of hire.
