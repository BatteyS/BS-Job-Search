Dear Hiring Team,

I am applying for the Director of Product Marketing role. I lead product marketing for a B2B supply-chain analytics platform, and I want to lead a product marketing team at a company that is past finding product-market fit and now has to explain itself to a bigger market.

When we repositioned Brightline's core platform, sales wanted a new tagline. I interviewed 38 customers and prospects who had chosen a competitor instead, and the answer was not the tagline: buyers wanted the software to make a decision for them. We rebuilt the story around decision automation, and our win rate against the main competitor rose from 24\% to 37\% over the next three quarters.

The 2024 pricing redesign started as a fight about discounts. Our win/loss interviews showed that per-seat pricing punished our best customers for adding users, so I made the case for usage tiers with the heads of product and finance. Average contract value rose 18\% the following year, and churn did not move. As you move more of your platform to usage-based pricing, I would bring that experience to it.

I hired the two product marketers I manage, and promoted one of them to senior in 2025. Leading a team of three, with a real voice in pricing, is the role I have been building toward.
