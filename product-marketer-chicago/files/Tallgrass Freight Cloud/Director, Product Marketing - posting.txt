Director, Product Marketing
Tallgrass Freight Cloud · Chicago, IL · Hybrid
$185,000 – $215,000

Tallgrass Freight Cloud gives shippers a live view of every load they move, and tells
their logistics teams what to do about the ones running late. More than 900 shippers use
the platform.

The role
You will lead product marketing for the platform: how we position it, how we launch, and
how we price. You will manage a team of three product marketers and report to the CMO.

What you will do
- Own positioning and messaging for the core platform, and keep it honest against what
  customers tell us.
- Run launches end to end, with product, sales, and customer success.
- Bring the buyer's view into pricing and packaging decisions, as we move more of the
  platform to usage-based pricing.
- Build a win/loss program and a competitive intelligence practice that sales actually
  uses.
- Hire, coach, and grow a team of three product marketers.

What you bring
- Eight or more years in B2B product marketing, including leading a team.
- A record of positioning that moved win rates, and launches that moved pipeline.
- Experience with a usage-based pricing model.
- Supply chain, logistics, or analytics software experience is a strong plus.
