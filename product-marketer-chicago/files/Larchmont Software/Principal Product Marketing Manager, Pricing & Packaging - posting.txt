Principal Product Marketing Manager, Pricing & Packaging
Larchmont Software · Remote (US)
$172,000 – $190,000

Larchmont Software makes operations analytics for manufacturers and distributors.

The role
You will lead pricing and packaging research across our operations analytics suite,
working with product, finance, and sales. This is an individual-contributor role
reporting to the VP of Product Marketing.

What you will do
- Run pricing research: customer interviews, surveys, and win/loss analysis.
- Recommend packaging and price changes, and model their effect with finance.
- Keep a current view of competitors' pricing.
- Launch pricing changes to sales and to customers.

What you bring
- Seven or more years in B2B product marketing or pricing.
- Experience leading a pricing or packaging change from research to launch.
- Comfort with SQL or a BI tool for your own analysis.
