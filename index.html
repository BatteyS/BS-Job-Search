BS Job Search

A job search workflow for Claude Code. It scans job boards, scores each role against what you want, and builds a tailored resume and cover letter for the ones you mark. It comes with a page that runs on your own computer, for reading and marking digests and finding what was filed.

Below is that page, filled in for two fictional people. Click through a digest, open a resume or a letter, and read the profile it was all scored against. Nothing you do here is saved.
